Zoom out to zoom inUnbucketing your map…Floating up to your map
  • Shows only while the map and its first pictures are still arriving. On fast Wi-Fi it is gone in under a second; on a slow 2G or 3G line the balloon keeps rising, one balloon after another, until everything is ready.
  • Clouds drift past downwards, so it feels like climbing. The balloon sways a little and the burner flickers.
  • When the page is ready the sky fades away and the map is already there underneath, so nothing jumps.
  • People who switch off motion on their phone see a still balloon instead.
